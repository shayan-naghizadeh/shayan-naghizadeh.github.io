IEEE PUBLICATION · ICSPIS 2025

Low-Precision POSIT Arithmetic for Spiking Neural Networks with Kahan Summation

Abstract. This work investigates low-precision Posit arithmetic for event-driven spiking neural networks, where reduced bit-width can make synaptic accumulation less stable. It applies Kahan compensated summation to recover lost accumulation precision and evaluates the approach as a hardware-efficient alternative to higher-precision arithmetic.

Abstract. This paper studies the edge deployment of quantized Transformer models for remaining useful life prediction. CETP: A Control-Encoded Tapered-Precision Number Format for Efficient Spiking Neural Network Inference

Research abstract. This manuscript explores CETP, a control-encoded tapered-precision number format designed to simplify numerical decoding for spiking neural network inference. The work investigates how numerical representation can be co-designed with neural dynamics and hardware cost in resource-conscious inference systems.

IEEE IKT 2025

Beyond One-Hot: CatBoost for Heating and Cooling Load Prediction

Abstract. This paper benchmarks regression and ensemble-learning approaches for heating and cooling load prediction on the ENB2012 dataset. It examines categorical-feature encoding strategies and shows how CatBoost can provide an accurate and interpretable alternative to conventional one-hot-based pipelines for building-load forecasting.
